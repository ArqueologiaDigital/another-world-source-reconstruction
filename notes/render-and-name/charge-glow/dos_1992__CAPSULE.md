# The actor charge glow -- one effect, three stages

The seven frames are **byte-identical in PRISON, CAVES and CAPSULE**, so
the effect is not stage-specific and the name carries no stage word. The
`PRISON_` prefix these had for an hour was mine, not earned, and is
dropped here rather than copied into two more stages --
`../crossstage-transfer/` holds stage-prefixed transfers precisely
because re-prefixing invents a scope, and removing it does not.
