## The three the art-transfer tool cannot express

`transfer_by_art.py` files a constant under "both already named" when the two
ports have each adopted a name for it, and skips it. These three are named on
both ports and the name on both is **wrong in the same way**: the pupil ladder
has seven rungs and only three were known when it was named, so 0, 1 and 2
were given to rungs 2, 6 and 7. The evidence is in
`proposals_chahi_amiga_1991__PRISON.md`; dos carries the identical ladder, at
the identical constants, and `canonical_md5` agrees for all three, so the
correction is the same correction.

| constant | proposed name | confidence | evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CINEMATIC_PRISON_WAKE_CLOSEUP_PUPIL_2 | CINEMATIC_PRISON_WAKE_CLOSEUP_PUPIL_6 | high | **renumber**: rung 7 of 7, `[0x16] > 0x06`. Same `canonical_md5` as amiga's, which measures 18x19 per eye and exactly fills the pupil plate's height |
| CINEMATIC_PRISON_WAKE_CLOSEUP_PUPIL_1 | CINEMATIC_PRISON_WAKE_CLOSEUP_PUPIL_5 | high | **renumber**: rung 6 of 7, `[0x16] <= 0x06`. Same `canonical_md5` as amiga's 16x17 |
| CINEMATIC_PRISON_WAKE_CLOSEUP_PUPIL_0 | CINEMATIC_PRISON_WAKE_CLOSEUP_PUPIL_1 | high | **renumber**: rung 2 of 7, `[0x16] <= 0x02`. Same `canonical_md5` as amiga's 8x9 |
