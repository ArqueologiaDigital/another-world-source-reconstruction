# CAVES (msdos): the same two families

Argument in `proposals_chahi_amiga_1991__CAVES_blast_and_flash.md`. The beam
tiles carry different constant numbers in msdos -- 774, 775, 776 against amiga's
749, 750, 751 -- which is the ordinary per-port indexing; the `canonical_md5`
and the draw order are the same. The wide flash keeps the same four numbers.
